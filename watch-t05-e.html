T05.E · Worked example

Count outcomes once, using complete cases

Worked example: a restriction that affects only one branch.

1:13 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

A wardrobe holds three different shirts and four different pairs of shorts. One shirt is red and two pairs of shorts are orange. How many outfits use one of each item and avoid pairing red with orange?

Check your reasoning

Without the restriction there are 3 × 4 = 12 outfits. Only the red shirt paired with either orange pair is forbidden. Removing those two outfits leaves 10.

Try a variation

A breakfast includes one bread and one topping. There are three breads and four toppings. One bread cannot go with two of the toppings. How many breakfasts are possible?

See the worked answer

The restricted bread has 2 topping choices. Each of the other two breads has 4. Adding the disjoint cases gives 2 + 4 + 4 = 10 breakfasts. Removing the two forbidden breakfasts from 12 gives the same result.

0:00 Choose a sandwich and a fruit

A lunch has one sandwich, cheese or bean, and one fruit, apple, pear or plum. Cheese may not be paired with plum. How many different lunches are possible?

0:14 Sort the lunches by sandwich

The restriction only mentions cheese, so split by sandwich. A lunch has exactly one sandwich, so these two cases cannot overlap.

0:23 List the fruits allowed with cheese

With cheese, plum is not allowed, so the allowed fruits are apple and pear. That is two lunches: cheese with apple, and cheese with pear.

0:33 Add the lunches with a bean sandwich

With bean, all three fruits are allowed. That is three lunches. Add the cases: two plus three is five.

0:40 Remove cheese with plum from the full menu

Check another way. Without the rule there would be two times three, six lunches. Exactly one of them, cheese with plum, is forbidden. Six minus one is five. Two methods agree.

0:53 Exclude the red-and-orange outfits

When branches have different sizes, add them rather than multiplying. Rebuild the tree yourself, then try this: there are three shirts and four pairs of shorts, but the red shirt cannot be worn with the two orange pairs. How many outfits are possible?
