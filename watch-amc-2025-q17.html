Read the solution

The top counts the five bottom positions 1, 2, 3, 2 and 1 times. Put 5 in the centre, 3 and 4 in the neighbouring positions, and 1 and 2 at the ends. One arrangement is 1, 3, 5, 4, 2. Its middle row is 9, 12, 11, so the top is 32 — answer C.

Why is this the maximum? If a bigger number is in a position counted fewer times than a smaller number, swapping them increases the total. Keep the biggest numbers in the most heavily counted positions; then no swap can improve it.
