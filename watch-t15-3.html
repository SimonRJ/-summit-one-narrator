T15.3

Pair objects or classify their local structure

Count quickly by pairing every object with a partner.

2:01 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

What is the sum of the whole numbers from 1 to 40?

Check your reasoning

20 pairs each adding to 41: 820.

Try a variation

Eight switches can each be on or off. How many settings have an odd number on?

See the worked answer

There are 2⁸ = 256 settings. Flip a particular switch to pair each setting with a different setting of the opposite parity. Exactly one member of each pair has an odd count, giving 128.

Read the transcript

0:00 Match each object with an opposite-type partner

If you can match every object with exactly one partner of the opposite type, then exactly half the objects are of each type. A good pairing lets you count without listing anything.

0:11 Explain why a pairing gives equal counts

Pairing arguments are short and they are proofs. They explain why the answer is half, instead of just noticing it in a few examples.

0:20 Count even settings of three switches

Three light switches can each be on or off, giving eight settings. In how many of them is an even number of switches on? Zero counts as even. Pause and try to predict the answer.

0:35 Flip the first switch to find a partner

Pair each setting with the one you get by flipping the first switch. Flip it again and you return, so the settings really are in pairs. No setting is its own partner, because flipping always changes something.

0:48 Compare odd and even settings within a pair

Flipping one switch changes the number of switches that are on by exactly one. So in each pair, one setting has an even number on and the other has an odd number. Four pairs, so four settings are even and four are odd.

1:02 Apply the switch pairing to ten switches

The same argument works whenever there is at least one switch to flip. With ten switches there are one thousand and twenty-four settings, and exactly half, five hundred and twelve, have an even number on. No listing needed.

1:16 Pair the ends of the list from 1 to 100

Pairing also adds. To add the numbers from one to one hundred, pair one with one hundred, two with ninety-nine, and so on. There are fifty pairs, each adding to one hundred and one. The total is five thousand and fifty.

1:30 Find the factor of 36 that pairs with itself

Always check that nothing is left without a partner, or paired with itself. The factors of thirty-six pair up to multiply to thirty-six, except six, which pairs with itself. That is why thirty-six has an odd number of factors.

1:45 Pair the numbers from 1 to 40

Find a rule that pairs every object with a different partner. If each pair has one of each type, the types occur equally often. Notebook challenge: what is the sum of the whole numbers from one to forty?
