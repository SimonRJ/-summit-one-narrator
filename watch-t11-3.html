T11.3

Organise counts, inventories and next-number

Count digits place by place, and know whether you are counting numbers or occurrences.

1:48 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

How many times is the digit 3 written in the numbers from 1 to 50?

Check your reasoning

Units: 3, 13, 23, 33, 43 (5 times); tens: 30 to 39 (10 times); total 15.

Read the transcript

"How many numbers contain a two?" and "How many twos are written?" are different questions. Twenty-two is one number, but it contains two twos. Decide which you are counting, then count one place at a time.

Counting straight through a long list is slow and error-prone. Counting each place separately is quick, and repeated digits take care of themselves.

All the whole numbers from one to one hundred are written out. How many times is the digit seven written? Pause and think about the units place first.

In the units place, seven appears once in every ten numbers: seven, seventeen, twenty-seven, up to ninety-seven. That is ten times.

In the tens place, seven appears in seventy to seventy-nine: ten times. Altogether the digit seven is written twenty times. Seventy-seven was counted once in each place, which is correct, because it has two sevens.

Now the other question: how many numbers contain a seven? The column has ten numbers and the row has ten, but seventy-seven is in both. Ten plus ten minus one is nineteen.

A related skill is counting all the digits. Numbering pages one to ninety-nine uses nine digits for the single-digit pages, and two digits each for the ninety pages from ten to ninety-nine. That is nine plus one hundred and eighty: one hundred and eighty-nine digits.

Decide whether you are counting numbers or occurrences. Count each place separately. For numbers, remove any overlap. Notebook challenge: how many times is the digit three written in the numbers from one to fifty?
