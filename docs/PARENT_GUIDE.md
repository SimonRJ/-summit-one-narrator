# Summit One parent guide

[Summit One](https://simonrj.github.io/-summit-one-narrator/) follows one sequence of 372 sessions, including 283 lessons, 53 MAWA-style investigation visits and 36 mixed workshops. The learner can complete multiple sessions on a day or leave gaps between sessions. Set aside about 30–60 minutes for a regular lesson, with more time available for an investigation when needed.

## Review the learner’s work

- **Profile** shows sessions completed and remaining. It also shows rank progress. The course sequence advances by completed sessions.
- **Completed sessions** shows the learner’s recorded answers and available lesson teaching. Completed session records are locked for review.
- **Evidence** distinguishes viewing an explanation, solving with support and solving independently. Ask the learner to show a notebook solution if you want to see the reasoning behind a recorded answer.
- **Exam results** show the submitted paper and individual results. Compare first-attempt accuracy and time on different original papers when judging progress on unfamiliar questions.

## Choose the right practice

Use Part A for speed and accuracy over questions 1–20 and Part B for harder questions 21–30. Rossmoyne practice has a separate section. The untimed MAWA-style investigations ask the learner to test cases, use diagrams or tables, and justify conclusions on paper.

Save authentic AMC papers for planned sittings. Confirm the learner has enough time before pressing Start, as each authentic paper has a single attempt. Any award estimate displayed by the app should be read as an estimate for practice.

The [current status](CURRENT_STATUS.md) records the number of available papers, the extent of the lesson audit and the video production status. The [student guide](STUDENT_GUIDE.md) gives the learner the steps to follow on an iPhone.
