## Required evidence before marking a film complete

1. Identify the actual film at its lesson route and its matching transcript and audio; do not substitute a similarly named script.
2. Review the whole film, including intermediate motion, and check that every illustrated quantity, colour, dimension, shape, condition, calculation and caption agrees with the spoken example.
3. Independently calculate the examples, including boundary cases. Ensure motion develops the mathematical idea, not merely a sequence of text cards.
4. Check caption readability and timing, one caption layer, and phone-sized playback. Preserve the audio where approved and correct.
5. Publish only the individually reviewed replacement, confirm its actual HTTPS playback, and record what was and was not verified. Do not call a file complete solely because it encodes, uploads, or plays.
