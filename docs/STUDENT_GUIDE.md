# Summit One student guide

Open [Summit One](https://simonrj.github.io/-summit-one-narrator/) on your iPhone and sign in with your learner account. Tap **Continue Session** to pick up your next lesson. The **Menu** at the top left opens your lessons, investigations, completed sessions and exams.

## A lesson

1. **Video** introduces the idea. Watch the available video or read the explanation. Pause and draw the example if it helps.
2. **Remember** brings back a tool you used before.
3. **Discover** teaches a method. Work through the quick decisions and the supported question.
4. **Try** lets you practise the method yourself. Work on paper, then type your final answer.
5. **Transfer** gives you an unfamiliar problem. Decide which method to use. After submitting, open the worked solution and compare the steps.
6. **Check** brings together the work from the session. Complete and lock it when you are finished. Use **Completed sessions** to reopen saved work and lesson teaching.

A lesson usually takes 30–60 minutes. Follow the session order at your own pace. The path includes a MAWA-style investigation every seventh session. Use a notebook for its diagrams, cases and explanations.

## Practice and exams

| Menu item | What to expect |
|---|---|
| AMC Practice · Part A | 20 multiple-choice questions. Aim for 20 correct in 20 minutes. |
| AMC Practice · Part B | 5 multiple-choice and 5 integer-answer questions. Aim for 10 correct in 40 minutes. |
| Real AMC exams | Full authentic Upper Primary papers. Read the warning before starting your single attempt. |
| Rossmoyne Honours | Practice papers for specialist mathematics entrance preparation. Check the instructions on the paper you choose. |

Press **Save** after typing an integer answer. Before submitting, check the list of unanswered questions and unsaved answers. After a paper, use the results to choose one or two problems to solve again in your notebook.
