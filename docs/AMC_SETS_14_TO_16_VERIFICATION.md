The AMC exam centre has six active single-attempt papers in these three sets. Part A gives pupils 20 minutes for 70 marks. Part B gives pupils 40 minutes for 65 marks.

All 90 answers passed separate mathematical checks. These included exhaustive enumeration, probability by counting equally likely outcomes, coordinate calculations, graph searches and exact arithmetic. Each question includes its own worked explanation and a learning skill. Reviewers compared close matches by both wording and mathematical task. Revised items went through the comparison again. The published editions contained no exact matches to earlier question stems.

Two temporary authenticated sittings tested each of the six papers. Submitting a complete correct response set earned full marks. Omitting the final response reduced the mark by precisely its value. Each test verified the timer, submitted report, solutions, skill feedback, locked answers and repeat-attempt behaviour. The test sittings were rolled back.

Readback matched the complete private editions field by field. The stored reference fingerprints for the 19 authentic papers, 570 authentic questions and 152 linked problems were identical before and after each release.
