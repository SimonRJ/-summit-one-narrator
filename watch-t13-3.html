T13.3

Count hidden pieces through layers and boundaries

Count cubes in a solid by layers, including the ones you cannot see.

2:04 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

A 4 by 4 by 4 cube is painted and cut into unit cubes. How many have no paint?

Check your reasoning

The inner 2 by 2 by 2 block: 8.

Read the transcript

0:00 See a block as horizontal layers

A drawing of a solid only shows some of its cubes. The rest are hidden behind or underneath. To count them all, slice the solid into layers. Count one layer, then count the layers.

0:12 Include cubes hidden in the drawing

Counting only visible cubes can miss part of a solid. Counting layer by layer is reliable, and it also answers questions about which cubes are on the outside.

0:22 Inspect a four-by-three-by-two block

A solid block is four cubes long, three cubes wide and two cubes high. How many small cubes does it contain? Pause and picture one layer.

0:35 Build the block from two layers of twelve

One layer is four by three, which is twelve cubes. There are two layers, so the block contains twenty-four cubes. Half of them are in the bottom layer, including some that were hidden in the drawing.

0:47 Peel away the painted outside of a cube

Now a harder question. A three by three by three cube is painted all over, then cut into twenty-seven small cubes. How many have no paint at all? Only a cube that touches no outside face. Peel off the outer layer in every direction, and a single cube is left in the very centre. The answer is one.

1:07 Count one centre cube on each of the six faces

You can sort all twenty-seven. Eight corner cubes have three painted faces. Twelve cubes along the edges have two. One cube at the centre of each face has just one painted side. There are six faces, so there are six such cubes altogether. One in the centre has none. Eight, twelve, six and one make twenty-seven, so nothing is missing.

1:30 Stack nine, four and one into a staircase

Layers also handle uneven solids. A corner staircase has nine cubes on the bottom layer, four on the next and one on top: fourteen altogether. Count each layer as it really is.

1:43 Find the unpainted core of a four-cube-wide block

Slice the solid into layers. Include the hidden cubes. If you sort the cubes into types, check that the types add up to the total. Notebook challenge: a four by four by four cube is painted and cut into small cubes. How many small cubes have no paint?
