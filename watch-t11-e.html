Add fifty-eight and sixty-seven, and explain what each carry really means.

0:09 Exchange ten units for one ten

In the units column, eight plus seven is fifteen units. Ten of those units make one ten. Carry that ten to the next column, and five units remain.

0:20 Exchange ten tens for one hundred

In the tens column there are five tens, six tens, and the carried ten: twelve tens. Ten of those tens make one hundred. Carry it, and two tens remain.

0:31 Read the new hundreds digit

The answer is one hundred, two tens and five units: one hundred and twenty-five. Neither starting number has a hundreds digit. The hundreds digit in the answer exists only because of the second carry.

0:44 Check 125 by rounding and regrouping

Check two ways. Roughly, sixty plus seventy is one hundred and thirty, which is close. Exactly, fifty plus sixty is one hundred and ten, eight plus seven is fifteen, and together they make one hundred and twenty-five.
