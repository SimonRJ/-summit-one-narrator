T06.3

Turn contribution limits into a sharp bound

Turn "each group uses up some of a limited supply" into a maximum, then reach it.

2:05 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

Six students; each day 3 games; no pair plays twice. Largest number of days?

Check your reasoning

15 pairs / 3 per day = 5 days. One schedule for A to F is AF, BE, CD / AE, DF, BC / AD, CE, BF / AC, BD, EF / AB, CF, DE. Each day's games are disjoint and each pair appears once.

Read the transcript

If every group uses up some of a limited supply, the supply limits how many groups you can have. Twelve tokens, three per group, means at most four groups. You know that before making a single group.

This gives the first half of a "largest possible" answer: a ceiling. The second half is showing that the ceiling can actually be reached. A complete solution needs both.

Five students are in a club. Each day, two pairs work together and one student rests. No two students may be partners more than once. What is the largest number of days this can continue? Pause and decide what is being used up.

The supply is pairs of students. Five students form ten different pairs. Each day uses two new pairs. So there can be at most five days.

Can five days really be done? Here is a schedule. Day one: A with B, C with D. Day two: A with C, B with E. Day three: A with D, C with E. Day four: A with E, B with D. Day five: B with C, D with E. All ten pairs appear exactly once.

What if each group has three people? Counting pairs gives a ceiling of three groups. But any two legal groups must share exactly one person. Two triples cannot be disjoint among five people, and sharing two would repeat a pair. For a third group, using the shared person repeats a pair with any other person. Leaving that person out forces three choices from the two outer pairs, so one pair repeats. Two groups fit, and a third never does.

Find the limited supply. Divide to get a ceiling. Then either build an example that reaches it, or explain why it cannot be reached. Notebook challenge: six students play chess, one game per pair at most. Each day three games are played. What is the largest possible number of days?
