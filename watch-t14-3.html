T14.3

Read a schedule as groups of nonconflicting edges

Turn a timetable problem into a network and find the fewest time slots.

2:03 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

Six players each play every other player once. A round has at most 3 games and no player plays twice in a round. What is the fewest rounds?

Check your reasoning

There are 15 games, so at least 5 rounds. One schedule is AF, BE, CD; BF, AC, DE; CF, BD, AE; DF, CE, AB; EF, AD, BC.

Try a variation

Two red players must each meet three blue players once. Write the red players as A and B and the blue players as X, Y and Z. What is the fewest rounds if each player can play once per round?

See the worked answer

A has three different opponents, so at least 3 rounds are needed. Use AX with BY, then AY with BZ, then AZ with BX. Every required pair occurs once and nobody plays twice in a round.

Read the transcript

0:00 Draw a network of shared meetings

A timetable can be drawn as a network. Each person is a dot, and each meeting that must happen is a line between two dots. Meetings in the same time slot must not share a person. So a time slot is a set of lines with no dot in common.

0:16 Read a clash in the network

Drawing it this way turns "avoid clashes" into something you can see. It also gives quick lower limits on the number of slots, before you build anything.

0:26 Schedule all the pairs of four players

Four players each play one game against every other player. A player can play at most one game per round. What is the smallest number of rounds needed? Pause and count the games.

0:41 Bound the rounds by games per round

There are six games. A round uses each player at most once, so it holds at most two games. Six divided by two is three. At least three rounds are needed.

0:53 Pair four players in three rounds

Can three be reached? Round one: A plays B, and C plays D. Round two: A plays C, and B plays D. Round three: A plays D, and B plays C. All six games are played. Three rounds is the answer.

1:08 Allow a resting player in a group of five

With five players there are ten games. In any round one player must rest, so again at most two games fit. That takes at least five rounds, even though each player plays only four games. The five-day pairing schedule from the earlier lesson shows that five rounds can be reached.

1:26 Use the busiest person to bound the slots

There is a second kind of limit. A person who is in three meetings needs at least three slots, whatever everyone else is doing. Use whichever limit is stronger, and then show a timetable that reaches it.

1:39 Schedule fifteen games for six players

Draw people as dots and meetings as lines. A slot is a set of lines sharing no dot. Find a limit, then build a timetable that reaches it.
