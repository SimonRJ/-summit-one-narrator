T09.E · Worked example

Make fractions, decimals and algebra agree

Worked example: equal trays, each with a pouch and loose counters.

1:21 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

Four identical bags each contain one box and 3 loose marbles. The boxes contain equal numbers of marbles. There are 40 marbles in total. How many are in each box?

Check your reasoning

Each bag holds 40 ÷ 4 = 10 marbles altogether. Three are outside the box, leaving 10 − 3 = 7 marbles in each box.

Try a variation

Three identical boxes each contain a bag of marbles and 4 loose marbles. The boxes together hold 39 marbles. How many marbles are in each bag?

See the worked answer

Each box holds 39 ÷ 3 = 13 marbles. Subtract its 4 loose marbles to get 9 per bag. Check that 3 × (9 + 4) = 39.

0:00 Share 21 counters across three trays

Three identical trays each hold one pouch and two loose counters. Every pouch holds the same number of counters. Altogether the trays contain twenty-one counters. How many counters are in each pouch?

0:17 Find the total on each tray

The three trays are identical, so they share the twenty-one counters equally. Twenty-one divided by three is seven. Each tray holds seven counters.

0:28 Separate five counters inside the pouch

In one tray, two of the seven counters are loose. The rest are in the pouch. Seven minus two is five. Each pouch holds five counters.

0:37 Write the tray story as an equation

In symbols: three lots of a pouch plus two equals twenty-one. So a pouch plus two equals seven, and a pouch equals five. The bars and the equation tell the same story.

0:49 Remove all six loose counters first

There is a second route. Take away all six loose counters first. Fifteen counters remain, shared by three pouches: five each. Two different orders, the same answer, which is a good check.

1:02 Find the marbles inside each of four boxes

You can share first, or remove the extras first. Rebuild both routes, then try this: four identical bags each hold one box and three loose marbles, making forty marbles altogether. How many marbles are in each box?
