T12.3

Use coordinates or extra lines to make a proof visible

Add one helpful line, or use a grid, to make a hidden relationship visible.

2:01 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

On a grid, one side of a tilted square goes 2 squares right and 1 square down. What is its area?

Check your reasoning

Surrounding 3 by 3 square (9) minus four triangles of area 1: 5.

Read the transcript

Sometimes a diagram does not contain the line you need. You are allowed to add one. A good extra line connects what you know to what you want. A bad one just adds clutter, so add a line only when you can say what it is for.

Many geometry problems are one well-chosen line away from being easy. Learning which lines tend to help is a real skill: a parallel line, a height, or a line that makes a triangle with equal sides.

A path runs between two parallel lines, with a bend to the right of both ends. It makes thirty-five degrees with the top line and forty-five degrees with the bottom line. What is the angle at the bend? Pause and think about what line you could add.

Draw a line through the bend, parallel to the other two. It splits the bend angle into two parts.

The upper part matches the thirty-five degree angle, because of the parallel lines. The lower part matches the forty-five. So the angle at the bend is thirty-five plus forty-five: eighty degrees.

A grid is another way to make things visible. Here is a tilted square. One side goes three squares to the right and one square down. What is its area? Surround it with a four by four square, area sixteen. The four corner triangles each have area one and a half, six altogether. Sixteen minus six is ten. The tilted square has area ten.

The most common helpful line of all is a height. Dropping a perpendicular splits an awkward triangle into two right-angled ones, which are much easier to work with.

Add a line only when you can say why. Parallel lines carry angles; heights create right angles; a grid makes areas exact. Notebook challenge: on a grid, one side of a tilted square goes two squares to the right and one square down. What is its area?
