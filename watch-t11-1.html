T11.1

Translate position into value

Write a number in terms of its digits so that place value can be used.

1:56 · Full HD

Try it in your notebook

A two-digit number equals 4 times the sum of its digits. Find all such numbers.

Check your reasoning

10A + B = 4A + 4B gives B = 2A: 12, 24, 36, 48.

Try a variation

A two-digit number exceeds its reversal by 54 and its digits add to 10. What is it?

See the worked answer

If the tens and ones digits are a and b, reversing changes the number by 9(a − b). Thus a − b = 6 and a + b = 10. The digits are 8 and 2. Check that 82 − 28 = 54.

Read the transcript

0:00 Compare the value of 4 in 42 and 24

The digit four in forty-two is worth forty, because of where it stands. Swap the digits and the same four is worth only four. A digit's position multiplies its value, and writing that down turns digit puzzles into simple algebra.

0:16 Write a two-digit number using place value

Digit puzzles look like guessing games. But if you write a two-digit number as ten times its first digit plus its second, most of the guessing disappears.

0:26 Reverse a number to gain 27

A two-digit number is reversed, and the new number is twenty-seven bigger. What can you say about its digits? Pause and write both numbers using place value.

0:39 Express both digit orders with A and B

Call the tens digit A and the units digit B. The number is ten A plus B. The reverse is ten B plus A.

0:48 Factor the difference between the numbers

Subtract. The difference is nine B minus nine A, which is nine times the gap between the digits. Twenty-seven is nine times three. So the units digit is three more than the tens digit.

1:02 List and check the possible originals

That gives fourteen, twenty-five, thirty-six, forty-seven, fifty-eight and sixty-nine. Check one: fifty-two minus twenty-five is twenty-seven.

1:14 Add a number to its reverse

Adding a number to its reverse gives eleven A plus eleven B: eleven times the digit sum. Forty-seven plus seventy-four is one hundred and twenty-one, which is eleven times eleven.

1:27 Use the limits on each digit

Remember that a digit is not just any number. The first digit runs from one to nine, and the others from zero to nine. Those limits often finish a puzzle.

1:37 Find numbers four times their digit sum

Write numbers using place value. Reversing and subtracting gives a multiple of nine. Reversing and adding gives a multiple of eleven. Notebook challenge: a two-digit number is four times the sum of its digits. Find all such numbers.
